The benchmark

A common interface.
A broad test of decisions.

MMDM evaluates text-based System-1 Decision Models that assign probabilities to the candidates in a request. It brings together knowledge, logical and quantitative reasoning, policies, planning, preference, and scoring—including decisions over evolving states and long contexts.

01

Open-vocabulary options

Each request defines its own candidates and criteria. Option names carry meaning, with decision spaces ranging from 2 to 77 choices.

02

Interacting decisions

Evidence, rules, and even the options themselves can depend on one another. The model must interpret the complete decision context.

03

Correctness and uncertainty

4,351 hard targets test definite decisions. 610 soft targets test probability estimates, including uncertain judgments on explicit score scales.

How to read these results

Hard Accuracy ↑ measures how often the highest-probability option matches the hard target. Brier ↓ is the sum of squared errors between predicted and target probabilities, averaged over the evaluated items.

The overall Brier columns use a common valid cohort. Category Brier uses each model’s valid outputs in that category; the table reports the corresponding counts. Model names link to their source or model card.

MMDM’s public test combines the 1,100-item development and 3,861-item test partitions used during OpenJev development. These historical roles are retained in the dataset documentation.
